Company Policy Handbook (Sample)

1. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 14 days of annual leave per year. Sick leave is up to 14 days per year with a medical certificate. Parental leave is 60 days for primary caregivers and 30 days for secondary caregivers.

2. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote days must be logged in the HR system before 10:00 AM on the working day. Office attendance is required every Tuesday for team collaboration.

3. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses up to RM 500 require only a receipt. Expenses above RM 500 require pre-approval from the department head. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

4. Security Policy
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) on company systems. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Sharing credentials is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

5. AI Usage Policy
Employees may use approved generative AI tools for work. Outputs that contain personal data or confidential information must be reviewed by a human before use. AI-generated code must be reviewed and tested before deployment. Employees must not enter confidential company data into public AI tools.
